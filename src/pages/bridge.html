1.0 Three clocks

His working day runs on three clocks: at 9 a.m. in Casablanca it is 4 p.m. at the partner university in Beijing and the middle of the night in New York. Nabil keeps the conversation going between all three.

2.0 What the work is

He takes a student from the first question to enrollment: choosing a university and a program, checking entry requirements, comparing tuition and scholarships, and filing the applications. He speaks for the student and the family with the partner universities. His advice affects a family's savings and four years of a student's life.

3.0 Why it belongs in a finance portfolio

A client-facing finance job asks for the same skill: explain a decision to two parties who know different things and want different things, and keep the trust of both. The work also gives him first-hand knowledge of emerging markets, because he spends his evenings on the phone with families who live in them.
