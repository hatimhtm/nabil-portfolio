Trading qualification: Bulenox, 50K program

Bulenox is a futures trading firm that funds traders who get through its evaluation. Bulenox gave Nabil this Certificate of Achievement “in recognition of excellent performance in the Bulenox Qualification Program” on a 50K account.

The program tests how a trader sizes positions and respects loss limits, which a trading desk also checks before it hires.
