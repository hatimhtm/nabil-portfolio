Design-Mall

I co-founded a digital marketplace as a student in Morocco. I handled product, marketing and growth, then pitched it to judges and investors, who picked it for the Top 50 University Startups.

1.0 The situation

Independent designers and small creators in Morocco had products to sell, but each was too small to build an audience and handle logistics alone. The Design-Mall team bet on one marketplace that gathers their catalogues and does the digital marketing for all of them.

2.0 What I did

As co-founder and digital strategist I coordinated the launch and handled product decisions, marketing and growth. I ran the social-media channels that brought the marketplace its users. I then pitched the venture to judges and investors at UM6P Explorer, and the jury picked it for the Top 50 University Startups.

3.0 What I took from it

I took four lessons from the venture that apply to an analyst's work:

Lesson from the ventureHow it helps on a finance desk
The first customers changed the plan.I treat a financial model as a guess to test against what buyers do.
Product, marketing and finance depend on each other.I read a company by looking at how its functions fit together, because I have run all three at once.
Revenue told me what worked.I look at cash flow before I listen to the story.
I pitched to a jury of investors.I have defended a thesis in person to people deciding whether to back it.
