I'm Nabil — a finance and business-administration student from Ifrane, Morocco, now based in Manhattan. Last summer went to market research for a digital-asset trading firm. Evenings and weekends go to the projects in the coverage universe — one of which now runs daily inside a New York clinic.

The plan from here is explicit: a graduate finance degree in New York, then a research or analytics desk where the data toolkit and the global lens both earn their keep.

Trading qualification — Bulenox, 50K program

Bulenox is a futures trading firm that funds traders who get through its evaluation. The Qualification Program is that evaluation: reach the profit target while staying inside the firm's risk rules. The firm issued NM this Certificate of Achievement “in recognition of excellent performance in the Bulenox Qualification Program” on a 50K account.

It is on a finance report because a program like this does not grade opinions about the market. It grades position sizing, loss limits, and patience — the habits a desk hires for.
