1.0  Who this is

I'm Nabil — a finance and business-administration student from Ifrane, Morocco, now based in Manhattan. I began my degree at Al Akhawayn University, spent an exchange year at Monroe College in New York on a J-1 (and made the Dean's List), then transferred to Mercy University to finish my B.S. in Finance in the city I'd bet everything on. First semester after the transfer: Dean's List again.

By day I study markets; since October 2025 I've also held a market research seat at Prometheus Trading, turning economic data into reports that support live decisions. Evenings and weekends go to the projects in the coverage universe — one of which now runs daily inside a New York clinic.

The plan from here is explicit: a graduate finance degree in New York, then a research or analytics desk where the data toolkit and the global lens both earn their keep.
