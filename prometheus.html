1.0 The brief

A market maker earns its living by quoting both sides of a market, on exchanges that never close. Prometheus Trading does that in digital assets. A firm in that seat needs two things from research: to know what changed this week before the screen says so, and to know where it stands against everyone else quoting the same markets. That was the project — twelve weeks, June to August 2026.

2.0 What NM did

Three workstreams ran side by side, and a fourth closed the file:

4.0 Why it sits on a finance book

Because this is the entry-level job on a research desk, done for a firm with money at risk. Each part of the summer maps onto work a junior analyst is handed in the first month:

What the summer trainedWhere it pays on a desk
Writing to a weekly deadline.Morning notes and weekly wraps are the first things a junior analyst owns.
Reading catalysts, sentiment, and flows together.No single feed explains a move. The job is the cross-read.
Benchmarking competitors on product and positioning.Point the same grid at listed companies and it is a comps table.
Closing with one strategy report.Research ends in a recommendation, or it was only reading.
A weekly brief is a view with a date on it. A summer of them is a record of how someone thinks.
