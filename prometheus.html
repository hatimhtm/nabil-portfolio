1.0 The brief

A market maker quotes a buy price and a sell price all day and earns the difference; Prometheus Trading does that in digital assets, on exchanges that stay open around the clock. The firm wanted two things from research: a read on what changed each week, and a picture of where it stands against other firms quoting the same markets. I worked on both for twelve weeks, from June to August 2026.

2.0 What I did

I ran three workstreams side by side and finished with a fourth:
