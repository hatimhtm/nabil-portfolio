Design-Mall

Nabil co-founded a digital marketplace as a student in Morocco. He handled product, marketing and growth, then pitched it to judges and investors, who picked it for the Top 50 University Startups.

1.0 The situation

Independent designers and small creators in Morocco had products to sell, but each was too small to build an audience and handle logistics alone. The Design-Mall team bet on one marketplace that gathers their catalogues and does the digital marketing for all of them.

2.0 What NM did

As co-founder and digital strategist he coordinated the launch and handled product decisions, marketing and growth. He ran the social-media channels that brought the marketplace its users. He then pitched the venture to judges and investors at UM6P Explorer, and the jury picked it for the Top 50 University Startups.
